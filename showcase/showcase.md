# Deepcore

You are the last prospector on a dead mining world. Above you: a derelict camp, a silent launch pad, and the promise of an escape rocket. Below you: a planet's worth of rock. Drill your own route through it, follow the mineral seams, and turn each haul into another chance to leave.

Depth is cheap. Height is expensive. Your drill can carry you down a shaft in seconds, but the climb home takes jetpack fuel—and every kilogram of ore makes it harder. Push for one more vein, or head back with enough charge to reach the market? An overloaded bay can leave you grounded until you abandon part of the prize.

The earth changes as you descend. Copper-brown soil gives way to cold gray rock, dark stone, and the red-lit shell of the Core. Watch for a pale breath of gas before the drill breaks through. Route around molten pools. Improve your hull, drill, tank, and lifting power, then fit a scanner and hunt down the exotic crystals your rocket needs.

Build the rocket one component at a time. The last piece demands a Core Sample—and extracting it starts a ninety-second countdown that follows you all the way home. Choose a short descent or a marathon, keep a checkpoint in Standard or risk everything in Hardcore, and decide how much this next trip is worth.
